# Northwind Industrial Supply

Northwind Industrial Supply is a regional distributor of motor oil, heavy-duty
diesel oil, transmission fluid, and hydraulic fluid serving auto shops,
dealerships, and fleets across the metro.

## What we offer
- Several hundred SKUs across passenger, heavy-duty diesel, and transmission lines.
- Next-day delivery across the metro on stocked items.
- Net-30 terms for qualified accounts.
- Bulk supply by the drum and by the tote for high-volume shops.

## How we handle pricing
We do not publish prices. Every quote is built per account based on volume and
delivery cadence. When a shop asks for pricing, we send a written quote within
one business day. Never state a number without a quote on file.

## Contact
Accounts are handled by the Northwind sales desk. A real person follows up by
phone on every new account.
